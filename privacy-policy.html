LocalGames – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

LocalGames ("the app") is developed by Simple and Small Apps. This policy explains what happens with data when you use the app. Short version: the app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and does not connect to the internet.

Data that stays on your device

Settings (language, theme), favorites, scores and game progress are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Playing together on a local network

When you turn on sharing or start a local hotspot, the app runs a small web server on your phone. Other people on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot can open it in their browser. The player names, chat messages and game moves they enter are sent only to your phone over the local network, kept in memory while the game runs and deleted when sharing stops or the app is closed. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. As the host you can turn chat off, set a room PIN and remove players.

Permissions

  • Network / Wi-Fi state – to show your local address and QR code and to run the local server.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices (Android 13+) or location (Android 8–12) – required by Android to start a local hotspot. It is asked only when you start the hotspot. The app never reads or stores your location.
  • Notifications and foreground service – to show a notification while sharing is on, so games keep running for your guests.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
